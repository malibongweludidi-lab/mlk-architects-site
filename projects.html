the work

schools, homes and community buildings — shown in our own renders.

the case studies below are drawn from the practice's design visualisations. each one follows the brief through to the design response and the material thinking behind it. filter by sector, or read straight through.

nikela primary school — design render
educational — r 80m

nikela primary school

location — kwazulu-natal
status — appointment of architect, ipac assessment
client — department of education, kzn
typology — primary education campus

context

a full primary-school campus on an open rural site — the brief asked for teaching, assembly and sports facilities that could grow into a recognisable centre for the surrounding community.

response

long classroom wings frame a central courtyard and a tiered assembly amphitheatre, with a sports field and hard courts set to one side — the plan reads clearly from the air and on the ground.

materiality

face-brick wings under pitched charcoal roofs, paired with shaded walkways and rainwater tanks — durable, low-maintenance and suited to a school's daily wear.

phuthini secondary school — design render
educational — r 65m

phuthini secondary school

location — kwazulu-natal
status — appointment of architect, ipac assessment
client — department of education, kzn
typology — secondary education campus

context

a full secondary-school campus organised as a family of lettered teaching blocks, where the brief asked for a clear, legible plan that pupils could navigate easily and that could grow into a recognisable place.

response

classroom wings are grouped into named blocks — a, b, c, d — each marked by a bold green portal and signage, and linked by wide covered walkways that wrap a series of brick-paved courtyards and play spaces.

materiality

face-brick wings under charcoal roofs, bright green accent gateways, and patterned brick-and-stone paving — durable, low-maintenance and full of colour cues that help young pupils find their way.

old mill estates residence — interior render
residential — private home

old mill estates residence

location — old mill estates
status — design & visualisation
client — private
typology — single-family home

context

a generous family home on a landscaped estate plot, where the brief asked for open, sociable living spaces and a strong indoor-outdoor relationship around a pool court.

response

an open-plan lounge, dining and kitchen flow out to a braai patio and pool, with bedrooms held in their own quiet wing — all under a low, tiled hipped roof.

materiality

warm plaster and natural stone outside; honed marble counters, timber screening and pale timber floors inside — a calm, durable palette for daily family life.

house zungu — night render
residential — private home

house zungu

location — private estate
status — design & visualisation
client — private
typology — double-storey contemporary home

context

a contemporary double-storey family home where the brief called for bold, modern living spaces, strong street presence and a close relationship between the upper living level, the balconies and the pool below.

response

a stacked composition lifts the main living and bedrooms to a glazed upper floor that opens onto wraparound balconies, while the ground level holds the entrance, garages and pool terrace.

materiality

off-shutter and plastered volumes, stacked-stone cladding, timber-lined soffits and frameless glass balustrades — a crisp, modern palette lit dramatically after dark.

ubuntu student accommodation — design render
residential — student accommodation

ubuntu student accommodation

location — urban infill site
status — design & visualisation
typology — student housing & ground-floor retail

context

well-located, well-run student accommodation in a dense urban setting, where the demand is for compact, affordable units that still feel part of a community.

response

studio units wrap a planted, landscaped courtyard that gives residents a shared green heart away from the street, with active retail space at ground level.

materiality

robust dark facades and timber screening outside; efficient, hard-wearing finishes inside — chosen with high-occupancy living and whole-life running costs in mind.

farm house — night render
residential — country home

farm house

location — rural smallholding
status — design & visualisation
client — private
typology — single-storey country home

context

a low-slung family home on an open rural plot, where the brief asked for generous indoor-outdoor living and a building that would sit comfortably in a wide, quiet landscape.

response

a covered patio opens the living spaces fully to the garden, while a stone feature wall and timber screening anchor the entrance — best seen after dark, when warm interior light spills out across the paving.

materiality

charcoal plaster, natural stone cladding and a tiled hipped roof, lit by recessed downlights that turn the elevation into a lantern at night.

community church — design render
community — place of worship

community church

location — kwazulu-natal
status — design & visualisation
typology — church & community hall

context

a congregation needing a dignified, welcoming home — a main worship hall with supporting community spaces, built to a realistic budget.

response

a clean, modern street elevation organised around a central cross, opening to a generous double-height hall lit by full-height glazing and an exposed beamed ceiling.

materiality

face brick, timber panelling and off-shutter surfaces give the interior warmth and permanence, while the daylit volume keeps it open and calm.
