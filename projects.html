johannesburg — proposal
educational — proposal — r 70m

uj department of clothing & textile, and campus factory

location — johannesburg
status — proposal, conceptual design & design development
client — university of johannesburg
typology — tertiary education / industrial-academic

context

the university of johannesburg's fashion department was to be split across two sites — the main campus, with its production factory at uj soweto. the brief asked for both halves of the same discipline to read as one institution.

response

a design that lets students move conceptually from sketch to production — making the industrial process itself part of the architectural experience, and a visible part of campus life rather than something hidden away.

materiality

an emphasis on legibility and innovation — surfaces and circulation conceived to spark ideas in young students and the surrounding community, not just house a curriculum.

richards bay, kzn — current project
educational — current — r 107m

meerensee high school

location — richards bay, kzn
status — master planning, conceptual design, design development
client — department of education, kzn
partnership — kuppan jv mlk architects

context

a sloping site north of richards bay, where the brief called for new ways of building and conserving energy — and for spaces that support student life beyond the classroom.

response

three platformed courtyards step down the slope, each one shaped to host public speaking, speech and drama activity — turning circulation space into programme space.

materiality

solar panels are integrated from the outset, not added later — one of several measures aimed at long-term energy conservation and sustainability.

big 5 false bay, kzn — completed
health — completed — r 256m

jozini chc clinic

location — big 5 false bay, kzn
status — completed — master planning, conceptual design, design development
client — department of health, kzn
partnership — sakhisizwe architects jv mlk architects

context

the brief called for a full community health centre in an area that needed one — not an extension of an existing facility, but a new and complete one.

response

a cluster of purpose-built buildings: a main clinic, radiology and laboratory, a maternity and woman-and-child centre, tb and infectious-disease wards, dentistry and casualty, a pharmacy, and staff accommodation — each function given its own properly considered space.

materiality

planning prioritised separation of clean and contaminated routes through the site — a quiet but critical material and circulation decision in any healthcare building.

richards bay, kzn — completed
educational — completed — r 80m

mgezeni high school

location — ntambanana municipality, richards bay, kzn
status — completed — master planning, conceptual design, design development
client — department of education, kzn

context

a school where fet preparation and home economics needed dedicated, properly equipped space — not a converted classroom.

response

one of the first schools of its kind to combine mechanical, electrical and civil workshops with a working kitchen and restaurant inside the school itself — turning vocational training into a daily, visible activity.

materiality

workshop and kitchen wings were planned for durability under heavy daily use — robust surfaces and services able to take vocational-scale wear.

hibiscus coast municipality, kzn
civic — r 12m

gamalakhe service office

location — hibiscus coast municipality, kzn
status — service hall facility
client — hibiscus coast municipality

context

a community in need of a single, accessible point of contact for several government services at once.

response

a shared service hall housing the department of home affairs, social work services, a micro police station, day-care for orphaned children, and staff offices — one building doing several jobs without feeling crowded.

materiality

queueing and waiting areas were treated as primary spaces, not corridors — shaping the public's actual daily experience of the building.

hibiscus coast municipality, kzn
civic — r 15m

umzimkhulu service office

location — hibiscus coast municipality, kzn
status — service hall facility
client — hibiscus coast municipality

context

a sister brief to gamalakhe — the same need for consolidated community services, in a different part of the municipality.

response

the same core programme — home affairs, social work services, a micro police station, orphan day-care, staff offices — adapted to a different site and community profile rather than simply repeated.

materiality

consistency of construction approach across both service offices kept costs predictable for the municipality while still responding to local conditions.

pretoria west — new development
residential — new development

student apartments, pretoria west

location — lot 1523, wf nkomo street, pretoria west
status — new development
typology — residential / student accommodation

context

a growing demand for well-located, well-run student accommodation close to tertiary institutions in pretoria west.

response

compact, efficiently planned units organised for both privacy and shared community life — the model the practice is carrying into its residential work going forward.

materiality

durable, low-maintenance finishes suited to high-occupancy student living, chosen with whole-life running costs in mind.
