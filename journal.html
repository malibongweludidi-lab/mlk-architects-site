2026 — ongoing
projects

meerensee high school moves through design development

our current project — a r107m master-planning and design commission for the department of education, kzn, run as kuppan jv mlk architects — continues through design development. the brief centres on a sloping site, three platformed courtyards, and integrated solar power for long-term sustainability.

on practice
insights

why we still train technologists in-house

mlk architectural services has, since the start, acted as a training ground for young architectural technologists — employing postgraduates and giving them real responsibility on live projects, from site supervision to presentation. it's slower than hiring experience. it's also how the profession in this country gets the next generation it needs.

on practice
insights

designing public buildings on a sloping site

meerensee high school sits on a slope that most briefs would have asked us to flatten. instead, the design steps down it — three courtyards at three levels, each one doing double duty as outdoor teaching and gathering space. the topography became the plan, not an obstacle to it.
