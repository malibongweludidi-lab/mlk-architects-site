the studio

a practice founded to give back while building a nation.

mlk architectural services was established by the late mr. ml khumalo, who set out to serve the community through good design. trading as mlk retailing and building services (reg. 2016/206334/07), the practice was registered in 1997, began operating in 2007, and remains fully black-owned with level 2 b-bbee status.

we work in accordance with the south african council for the architectural profession (sacap), and act as a training ground for young architectural technologists.

how we work

care. integrity. responsibility.

"we will ensure our client's satisfaction is fulfilled through suitable products and professional planning and design."

01 — care

complete customer focus

every brief is treated as its own problem. we listen first, then design — and stay close to the project through construction and close-out.

02 — integrity

honesty, plainly practised

we work to sacap regulation and to the construction rules that govern our sites — and we say so, openly, in our paperwork and on our projects.

03 — responsibility

social, and environmental

sustainable design and low-carbon methods, a training ground for young technologists, and a long memory of the communities we build in.
